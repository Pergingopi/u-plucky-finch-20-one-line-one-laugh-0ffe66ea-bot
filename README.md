A single funny line, under 200 characters, written to make a reader laugh in one breath. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “One Line, One Laugh” bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`), atomically and with mode 0644.

## The line

> I asked the void for a sign, and it replied 'seen 3:42 AM'.

59 characters. The laugh lands inside the line: an existential plea
answered by the most mundane modern insult — a read receipt from
nothing, at 3:42 AM. No setup, no picture, no explanation needed, and
it survives a second read because the image (the void, online, ignoring
you) only gets funnier once you picture it.

## The three that lost

1. “My smart fridge put me on a diet, so I unplugged it and ate the
   evidence.” — Stock premise: the judgemental smart fridge has been
   joked about for years, and the punchline is the first one a reader
   guesses.
2. “The Wi-Fi went down, so I talked to my family — turns out they're
   nice people with terrible passwords.” — Two beats and a setup the
   reader has to supply; the outage-forces-family-time joke is
   well-worn, and the twist arrives late.
3. “I told my Roomba and my cat I'm the boss; they held a meeting and
   voted me out of my own living room.” — Longer, punchline lands at
   the end of a clause, and the pets-form-a-union angle is familiar.
